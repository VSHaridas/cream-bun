## Full challenge brief
AI Content Creator Marketplace
1. Background
Generative AI tools have created a new category of creative professionals, including AI filmmakers, AI animators, and generative content creators. However, brands and creative agencies still lack an effective way to discover, evaluate, and engage creators with proven AI-production skills.

Existing freelance marketplaces are not designed around AI-native creative workflows. Brands may struggle to verify creator capabilities, understand the tools and workflows behind generated content, manage licensing and commercial-use requirements, and collaborate efficiently through multiple generations and revisions.

2. Challenge Statement
Design and build an AI-native marketplace connecting AI creators with brands and creative agencies.

The platform should help brands:

Discover suitable AI creators
Publish creative briefs
Evaluate AI-specific portfolios
Manage engagements from discovery through delivery
3. Core Scope
Creator Profiles & AI Portfolios
Showcase videos, animations, graphics, and other AI-generated work, including:

Tools/models used
Skills
Specialization
Relevant workflow information
Brand / Agency Briefs
Allow brands to define:

Campaign requirements
Content type
Style
Format/aspect ratio
Commercial-use requirements
Creator Search & Filtering
Enable brands to search and filter creators by:

Skills
Specialization
Tools
Content type
Other relevant profile attributes
4. Bonus Features
Creator Verification Signals
Help brands trust creator claims through visible indicators for:

Verified tools
Workflows
Past work
AI-Assisted Brief Builder
Help brands turn a rough idea into a complete, structured creative brief.

5. Deliverables
Working prototype of creator profiles with AI portfolios, brief creation, and creator search.
Short note on the profile and brief data model, including tools, skills, formats, and commercial-use fields.
Short demo or a few slides showing the approach.
6. Evaluation Criteria — 100 Points
Criterion	Weight	What Judges Look For
Creator Profiles & AI Portfolios	30%	Rich profiles showcasing AI-generated work, tools/models, skills, specialization, and workflows
Brief Definition	20%	Briefs that clearly and completely capture content type, style, format/aspect ratio, and commercial-use requirements
Discovery & Filtering	25%	Accurate search and filtering by skills and other attributes; sensible handling of empty results
User Experience	15%	Easy flows for creators and brands; clear portfolio viewing
Presentation & Demo	10%	Working prototype demonstrated clearly through a short demo or a few slides
7. Bonus Credit
Bonus credit may be earned for:

Creator Verification Signals: Visible trust indicators for tools, workflows, and past work.
AI-Assisted Brief Builder: Creating structured briefs from a rough idea.
Bonus credit is added within the relevant evaluation criteria:

Verification → Creator Profiles & AI Portfolios
Brief Builder → Brief Definition
Bonus credit is capped at approximately 5–10 points in total.
